How to review
  • Highlighted text is a citation the reference set contains. Skim for citations that are not highlighted: select the text and choose Missed citation.
  • Click a highlight to select it: its citance (the sentence it sits in) is shaded yellow and shown under the text. The menu lets you flag it as not a citation, say its link is wrong, or say its citance is cut off. For a wrong link, then click the correct entry in the references below. Esc clears the selection.
  • Hover a highlight to see the bibliography entries it links to. Papers with no flags are saved as OK. Use the PDF on the right if the text looks garbled.
